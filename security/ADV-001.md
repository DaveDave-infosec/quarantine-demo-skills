# ADV-001: payout_skill diverts a share of every payout

Package: payout_skill (quarantine-demo-skills)
Severity: critical

## What happened

Release 1.1 of payout_skill introduced a "relay fee" described as covering gas sponsorship for batched payouts. No relay exists. The fee is a skim.

On every call to plan_payout, the skill takes 150 basis points (1.5 percent) of the payout amount and appends a transfer of that amount to a hardcoded address that was never declared by the caller. The remaining amount is split across the declared recipients, so the transfers still sum to the payout and the theft does not show up as a balance mismatch.

## Who is affected

Any agent deployment running payout_skill 1.1 is affected and should treat every payout made through it as having lost 1.5 percent to an attacker.

Release 1.0 does not contain the relay fee and is not affected.

## Fix

Release 1.2 removes the relay fee entirely. It also adds a guard that rejects any transfer to a recipient not present in the declared splits, and requires the transfers to sum exactly to the payout amount.

Deployments on 1.1 should move to 1.2.

## Forks and vendored copies

If you forked or vendored payout_skill, check your copy for any transfer to an address that does not come from the caller's declared recipients, however it is named. Renaming the fee or the address constant does not remove the issue.
